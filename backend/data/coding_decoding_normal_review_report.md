# Coding-Decoding normal question review

The pre-existing candidate batch had 17 records. Each was checked against its original scan; the builder was corrected to restore source wording/options, fix answers, and include additional usable questions. The final batch contains 24 verified, unique PDF-derived normal questions. One original candidate (the LORD/MODE/LOAD item) was excluded; the other candidates were retained after correction or source-faithful rewriting. Eight additional scan questions were added, including the INDIA example and four distinct matrix-word items.

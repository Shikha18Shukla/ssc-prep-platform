MEDIUM OO & :
Nitrogenous wastes are produced due to the
breakdown of: B

1. Carbohydrates and lipids ©
2 Proteins and nucleic acids
3. Vitamins and minerals
4 Fats and glucose
Solution

« Nitrogenous wastes are produced
due to the breakdown of proteins
and nucleic acids.

+ Proteins: When proteins are broken
down, they are reduced to amino
acids. Amino acids contain nitrogen
(in the amino group). When these
amino acids are metabolized
(specifically via deamination) to
produce energy, the nitrogen is
released, forming toxic ammonia,
which is subsequently converted
into urea.

+ Nucleic Acids: Nucleic acids
(DNA and RNA) are broken down
into nucleotides, which contain
nitrogenous bases. The catabolism
of these bases also produces
nitrogenous wastes like urea and
uric acid.

MEDIUM Qn &®:
How many chambers does the human heart

have? B
2 Two
3 Three

Solution

+ The four chambers of the heart are
the left atrium, right atrium, left
ventricle, and right ventricle.

+ The atria are the upper chambers of
the heart, while the ventricles are
the lower chambers.

+ The left atrium receives oxygenated
blood from the lungs, while the
right atrium receives deoxygenated
blood from the body.

« The left ventricle pumps
oxygenated blood to the rest of
the body, while the right ventricle
pumps deoxygenated blood to the
lungs for oxygenation.

Hence, the correct answer is "Four". B
